# Axiom Evaluation Selection and Onboarding Runbook

**State:** Private. No applicant contact or live access authorized.

## Monday close

At Monday, September 14, 2026 at 8:00 p.m. CDT:

1. Disable the public form UI.
2. Use the provider-recorded submission timestamp as the governing time.
3. Freeze the response set.
4. Assign non-reversible candidate IDs.
5. Keep identities, contact fields, optional case-study interest, and any unnecessary company prestige signals away from first-pass scoring.

## Tuesday review

1. Fail eligibility first: no decision authority, no single repeated lane, prohibited regulated judgment, credential request, deception, transactions, or insufficient ten-day availability.
2. Score the five operating dimensions using `06-BLINDED-REVIEW-SHEET.csv`.
3. Total the five operating dimensions to 100 points.
4. Build the full selected-and-alternate ranking with the fixed resolver order: higher total; stronger independently verified evidence quality; then, only if both are equal, Baton Rouge/fitness relevance; then higher safety/boundedness, lane clarity, repetition/evidence, outcome quality, and authority/access in that order; then earlier provider-recorded submission timestamp. Record the resolver used. No random or unrecorded reviewer choice.
5. Shortlist no more than six businesses.
6. Run the same fifteen-minute owner screen for each:
   - State the workflow trigger in one sentence.
   - State the terminal disposition in one sentence.
   - Name the current human owner.
   - Name one repeated failure.
   - Name the observable evidence available.
   - Confirm authority and ten-business-day availability.
   - Confirm no prohibited data or action is required.
7. Independent second review resolves evidence quality and close scores before the fixed resolver order is applied. Conflicted reviewers recuse.
8. Percy approves exactly two provisional selections and up to two active ranked alternates by 5:00 p.m. CDT.

## Wednesday onboarding

After separately approved notices, the two provisional selections and up to two active ranked alternates complete the same conditional readiness packet by 3:00 p.m. CDT:

1. review the evaluation agreement and accept that it becomes operative only if the business receives a final slot;
2. accept a business-specific information-boundary attachment;
3. identify authorized attendees;
4. lock one lane and one outcome;
5. confirm an approved evidence path and availability for a first working session.

At 3:00 p.m., verify the two provisional businesses against those gates. If one fails, Percy may separately approve promotion of the next fully ready alternate by rank. Lock both final businesses by 4:00 p.m. The two final businesses must complete by 6:00 p.m. CDT:

1. final acceptance of `05-EVALUATION-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md`;
2. final business-specific information boundary;
3. first working session booked.

No production account is connected during this campaign-onboarding gate. If access becomes necessary later, it receives its own scope and approval.
