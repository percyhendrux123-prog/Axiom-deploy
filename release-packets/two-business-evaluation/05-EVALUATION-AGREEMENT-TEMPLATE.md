## Sponsored scope

Axiom sponsors the evaluation fee in full. There is no automatic charge or continuation.

The evaluation includes:

1. current-state map from exact trigger to terminal disposition;
2. current human owner and repeated continuity failure;
3. permission map for read, classify, draft, queue, execute, and stop states;
4. failure surface and non-compressible human gates;
5. baseline and evidence contract;
6. synthetic or read-only evaluation when separately authorized;
7. final finding: `SCOPE_READY`, `HOLD`, or `REJECTED`;
8. a scope-ready operator contract when the evidence supports it.

## Excluded from the sponsorship

- Production deployment.
- Live writes, messages, transactions, purchases, or payments.
- Credentials or secrets in public forms, email, chat, or shared documents.
- Regulated medical, legal, credit, employment, insurance, or safety-critical judgment.
- Unbounded access to customer, employee, health, or financial data.
- Ongoing operation, support, hosting, or monitoring.
- Guaranteed automation, savings, time return, revenue, or implementation.

Any later access, production deployment, continuing operation, case study, or commercial proposal requires a separate written scope and Percy’s explicit approval.

## Information boundary

Before any business evidence is inspected, the parties write an evidence inventory identifying:

- exact source;
- data owner;
- sensitivity class;
- permitted read or synthetic use;
- prohibited fields;
- minimum retention;
- required redaction;
- who may inspect it;
- deletion or return date.

The business does not send passwords, API keys, recovery codes, payment data, customer message bodies, private employee records, health data, or other secrets through unapproved channels. When a source cannot be reviewed safely, Axiom uses a redacted sample or rejects that evaluation step.

## Human control

Hermes may map, classify, draft, test against synthetic/redacted evidence, and prepare recommendations inside the agreed lane. Hermes does not approve its own production access or action. Percy and the business owner remain the authorities for consequential action.

An unknown permission, changed target, missing evidence contract, inconsistent count, or unclear terminal state causes a hold—not a best guess.

## Deliverables and acceptance

The evaluation is complete when Axiom provides:

- lane map;
- permission and stop matrix;
- measurable-outcome contract;
- evaluation observations with evidence handles;
- final disposition and reasoning;
- bounded deployment scope only if the lane is `SCOPE_READY`.

A `HOLD` or `REJECTED` result satisfies the evaluation when it is evidence-backed. Selection does not guarantee implementation.

## Publicity

The evaluation is private by default. The optional checkbox in the application only permits Axiom to ask about a later case-study agreement. It is not consent to publish the business name, logo, data, quote, result, or workflow. Any public use requires a separate asset-specific agreement and Percy’s exact publication approval.

## Withdrawal and termination

Either party may stop the evaluation. Axiom may stop immediately for unsafe scope, misrepresented authority, prohibited data, requested deception, payment authority, unauthorized outreach, or failure to maintain the agreed information boundary. On termination, data is returned or deleted according to the evidence inventory and any legal-preservation requirement.
