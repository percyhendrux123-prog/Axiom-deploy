# Axiom — Two Sponsored One-Lane Evaluations

**Prepared:** September 13, 2026  
**State:** Private offer authority. No publication, prospect contact, access request, deployment, commercial quote, or client mutation is authorized by this file.

## Public promise

**Two businesses. One operating lane each.**

Axiom will sponsor the evaluation fee for two owner-led businesses. Each evaluation tests one repeated operating lane and produces one evidence-backed outcome: a scope-ready operational-agent contract or an honest rejection.

The evaluation does not promise automation, revenue lift, time savings, or autonomous action.

## Why this is the offer

The legacy Axiom site attempted to sell packages before the current agent operating model existed. This offer demonstrates the current model without giving away indefinite labor or placing a new business into production before its permissions and outcome can be measured.

## Included

- One owner/operator working session.
- Current-state map from trigger to terminal disposition.
- One repeated leak and current human owner.
- One operating lane and one primary measurable outcome.
- Read, classify, draft, queue, and execute permission map.
- Failure surface, stop conditions, and human approval points.
- Baseline and evidence contract.
- Synthetic or read-only evaluation when the business grants the required permission.
- Final one-page operator scope with `SCOPE_READY`, `HOLD`, or `REJECTED` finding.
- Ten business days from the first working session.

## Not included

- Production deployment.
- Credentials in the public application.
- Live writes or outbound communication.
- Financial transactions or payment authority.
- Broad “automate the business” projects.
- Regulated professional judgment.
- Indefinite agent operation.
- A guaranteed implementation, result, saving, or revenue outcome.

Any live access, deployment, ongoing operation, case study, or commercial proposal is separate, permissioned, and priced only after the evaluation.

## Fit

Strong fit:

- The applicant owns or directly operates a real business and can authorize the working session.
- One workflow repeats often enough to observe and describe.
- The workflow has a recognizable trigger and ending.
- A person currently owns the work.
- Failure can be observed without inventing a number.
- The business can complete the working session and provide bounded evidence within ten business days.

Hold or reject:

- One-time projects.
- “Automate everything.”
- No decision-maker participation.
- No current human owner or observable events.
- A request centered on medical, legal, credit, employment, or other regulated judgment.
- A request that requires payment authority, secret disclosure in the form, deceptive identity, cold outreach, or safety-control bypass.

Baton Rouge and fitness-adjacent businesses receive priority only when fit is otherwise equal.

## Selection rubric — 100 points

- Lane clarity: 25
- Repetition and evidence availability: 20
- Measurable outcome quality: 20
- Decision authority and ten-day access: 15
- Permission safety and boundedness: 20
- Baton Rouge/fitness relevance: zero points; use only after two eligible applicants have the same 100-point operating-fit score and equivalent evidence quality

Case-study willingness, company size, follower count, existing AI spend, and ability to buy later are not scored.

### Deterministic ranking and alternates

Rank every eligible applicant, including alternates, using this fixed order:

1. higher 100-point operating-fit total;
2. if totals tie, stronger independently verified evidence quality;
3. only when total and evidence quality are equal, Baton Rouge/fitness relevance;
4. if still tied, higher permission safety and boundedness, then lane clarity, repetition/evidence, measurable-outcome quality, and decision-authority/access, in that order;
5. if every factor remains equal, earlier provider-recorded submission timestamp.

Do not use a random drawing, follower count, charisma, company prestige, or a reviewer’s unrecorded preference. Record every applied resolver on the blinded review sheet before Percy approves the ranking.

## Data and permission

The application collects business identity, contact, workflow description, decision authority, and evidence availability. It does not request credentials, customer records, employee records, financial account data, health data, or secrets.

Application data is used only to administer this selection. Non-selected records are deleted 30 days after both evaluation slots are finalized, subject to legal-preservation needs. Public case-study permission is optional, separate, and not scored.

## Commercial boundary

The evaluation fee is sponsored in full. There is no automatic continuation or charge. A business may keep the evaluation deliverables and stop. If a lane reaches `SCOPE_READY` and both parties want deployment, Axiom prepares a separate private proposal with owner-approved implementation and operating terms. Legacy public prices are not inherited.
