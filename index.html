II

Method

Before a system runs, we write down what starts the work, what counts as done, and where it has to stop and ask.

  1. IIntakeA request, message or deadline enters the system.
  2. IIContextPrior notes and decisions are gathered first, so the owner is not asked to explain again.
  3. IIIPreparationThe draft, the edit or the next action is produced in full.
  4. IVDecisionAnything that commits the business waits for the owner.
  5. VRecordThe outcome is checked and written down, so the next session starts where this one ended.

III

Limits of authority

A draft is never an approval.

Delegated

Reading, sorting, drafting, editing, and checking the result.

Held for approval

Anything that sends, publishes, spends, or alters a customer record. Prepared completely, then held.

Reserved to the owner

Moving money, setting prices, and making commitments in the owner's name.
