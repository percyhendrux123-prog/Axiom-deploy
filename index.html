Evaluation terms and privacy

Terms

Axiom is sponsoring the evaluation fee for two selected businesses. Each evaluation runs for ten business days from its first working session and includes current-state mapping, permission and failure boundaries, one evidence contract, bounded synthetic or read-only testing when authorized, and a final scope finding.

No purchase is required. Selection is merit-based, not random. Lane clarity, repetition and evidence, outcome quality, authority and access, and safe boundedness are scored. Case-study willingness, business size, social following, existing AI spend, and ability to buy later are not scored.

Selection is provisional until the business accepts the evaluation agreement and information boundary and books the first working session by Wednesday, September 16, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. CDT. Axiom may move to a ranked alternate when a gate is incomplete.

No result, saving, revenue lift, implementation, or deployment is guaranteed. Any live access, deployment, ongoing operation, case study, or commercial proposal is separate, permissioned, and governed by a later agreement. Production activity and commercial terms require a separate private agreement.

Privacy

The form collects business identity, owner/operator contact, authority, workflow description, system categories, and evidence availability solely to administer selection and the sponsored evaluation. It does not request credentials, private customer or employee records, payment data, health data, or secrets.

Access is limited to authorized reviewers. First-pass review uses candidate IDs where practical. Non-selected application records are scheduled for deletion 30 days after both evaluation slots are finalized, except for a limited legal, security, or dispute hold.

Application data will not be added to a general marketing list. Optional case-study interest is separate, unscored, and is not final publication consent. Questions: command@deployaxiom.com.
