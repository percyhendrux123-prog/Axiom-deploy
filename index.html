Continuity is usually the failure.

Work begins in one inbox, moves through another tool, then disappears in a handoff. The missing piece is not a vague automation layer. It is an operator that can retain the lane's relevant context, prepare the next state, and leave judgment visible to the person who owns it.

A deployment is a unit of responsibility.

It is not a feature bundle. Before an agent operates, its job and limits are explicit.

Operating contract

One defined trigger, scope, terminal state, and accountable owner.

Context

The minimum durable record needed to continue the lane without re-explaining it.

Permissions

Specific authority to read, classify, draft, queue, or separately execute.

Evidence

A visible disposition that shows where the work went and why.

The boundary is part of the product.

An agent may read, classify, draft, queue, execute, or stop only inside an explicit operating contract. Execution is separately permissioned; it is never implied by a draft.
