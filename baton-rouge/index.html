Questions owners ask

Is this an answering service?

It does the answering, and then it does the part answering services usually don't: it writes the call down, sorts it, and gets the caller to a next step. You see every call.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. It says so at the start of the call. Anyone who wants a person can ask for you and get transferred.

What about the calls I already miss?

That's where we start. Missed calls and new leads first, then the next thing you keep having to remember.

What can it agree to for me?

Nothing that commits your business. It takes details and books time with you. Prices, promises and exceptions wait for your OK.
