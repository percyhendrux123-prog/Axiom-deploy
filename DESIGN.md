# Axiom Site Design Contract

## Mode and screen job

The Axiōma night look (A230, Percy's choice after seeing the A227 Phase 2 build: "that's Axiom ... this isn't anything to hide"), restrained in A413 toward the firm register: plain section labels instead of shell prompts, Geist for reading text, IBM Plex Mono kept for labels, logs, figures and code. A night page that a non-technical owner understands in five seconds: the headline and a plain-English panel say what Axiom does, what waits for the owner, and where it runs. The terminal styling is the frame, never the explanation. Static HTML and CSS; one deferred script upgrades the story to its interactive form.

The previous contract (stone and brass letterhead, Cinzel, no motion) is in git history at `5327f8f`. Inner pages keep that structure and copy and wear the night shell (`assets/axioma/pages.css`, loaded after `firm.css`).

## Primary action

Primary action everywhere on the homepage: `Book a 15-minute call` (the free Google booking page) in the bar and the hero (A409, 2026-11-14); the hero's second action is `Write a short brief` to `#contact`. Any link to `#contact` opens the brief form on arrival (`assets/site.js`), so it takes one tap; the form's own summary reads `Write a short brief` and hides once open. The form ships `open` in the HTML, so with JavaScript off a `#contact` link lands on the form itself; with JavaScript it starts folded unless asked for (A413). The contact heading reads `Book a call, or send a brief.`; booking is the filled button there too, the brief outlined. If the reply field gets a mobile number, the form says we reply by text to that number only and stop on STOP (the privacy policy says the same). No phone number anywhere. The contact section opens the brief form (native disclosure, A384): name, business, team size, how to reply, what is eating the week (required), and what helps with it today (optional, A413). Fields are 16px (no iOS zoom). It posts to Netlify Forms (free and unlimited on the credit-based plan, checked 2026-11-12) with a honeypot, no analytics, and lands on `/brief/received/` (noindex). Submissions alert AXIOM through a host-side poll of the Netlify API; no Netlify email notification is configured, so nothing goes to percy@. The founder line points to the brief, not the mailbox. One label per intent.

## Brand standard

- The mark (2026-11-14, Percy: "the triangle is the one"): the unseen triangle. Three stone `#F1EDE4` circles on ink/night, each cut by one corner of an upward equilateral triangle that is never drawn (Kanizsa illusory contour). Geometry: vertices (500,285), (720.8,667.5), (279.2,667.5) on a 1000 square, circle radius 145; each cut is the 60° sector toward the other two vertices. Light-on-dark is primary; two colors per surface. It is the avatar and favicon (`assets/avatar.svg`) and the hero mark (inline outlined paths, no live text, 96-120px: the illusion needs about 90px; 22px in the header bar beside the name, where it reads as the logo, not the illusion). Never draw the triangle's edges; never add glow, gradient or a third color. Source and meaning: `~/Desk/Axiom/Brand/2026-11-14-global-mark/DECISION.md`.
- The meaning, in these words only: Euclid's Elements opens with axioms (five postulates, five common notions); Book I Proposition 1 builds an equilateral triangle from two circles; Kanizsa (1955): the eye completes a shape nobody drew. The canon axiom stays word for word: "Nothing sends, spends, or spins without the one who holds the gate." Ask before inventing any new axiom.
- Symbols: one per idea. The mark and its Euclid figure (the name), the braille caduceus (the gate), the herm medallion (the stop). The construction-line divider glyph retired in A413; sections divide with a plain 1px rule. Thin rules, circles and intersections only; no robots, circuits, brains or glow.
- Palette (brand ink and stone, A413): night `#121316` (brand ink), panel `#17181B`, rule `#2C2D31`, dim `#96928A` (brand muted), mid `#CDC8BD`, text `#DEDAD1`, strong `#F1EDE4` (brand stone). Stone carries the page. The homepage and inner pages share these values.
- Signals only: red `#FF453A` stop, yellow `#FFCC00` caution, green `#30D158` go, pale gold `#EADFB8` whisper. Color means state, never decoration; signals appear only on the replay's `held` and `yes` and in the story and its key, and every signal carries a second cue (bold solid / wavy / dashed / dotted underline) plus a spoken word for screen readers.
- Type, all self-hosted with their SIL OFL licenses: Geist (headings and reading text, buttons, nav), IBM Plex Mono (section labels, logs, ledger, figures, code), Cormorant Garamond italic (the axiom only). Latin and Latin Extended subsets (Axiōma needs ō).
- Square edges, 1px rules, one bordered panel per idea, no shadows, no gradients, no glow, no rounded SaaS cards, no fake dashboards. Section labels are plain words in small mono capitals (`aria-hidden`), never shell prompts; every section has a plain-English heading. No `#` comment markers in visible text. Smallest type on any page is 12px.
- Axiōma is given away: say "the spec is CC0 and the code is MIT" wherever it is named.
- Never: prices, client names, client stories, invented metrics, emoji, hype, autoplay, smooth scrolling, boot screens on the business site.

## The medallion story

- The story is set in IBM Plex Mono 15px (A413): its live layer places one span per word and lights STOP at weight 600, which keeps the same advance width only in a monospace face.
- Markup is the source of truth: each paragraph on one line; marked phrases are `<span class="ax ax-LEVEL" data-axioma-level="LEVEL">…<span class="ax-sr"> (WORD)</span></span>`.
- No JS or reduced motion: static float with a circular `shape-outside`, every phrase lit, nothing focusable or moving.
- Motion allowed: `assets/axioma/home.js` loads `assets/axioma/story.js` when the story nears the viewport. The flow stays in the DOM as screen-reader copy; an `aria-hidden` word layer is drawn; the medallion becomes a round button (drag, arrow keys, Shift, Home) with a 2px focus ring. Pretext (MIT) does the line breaking. Source and rebuild steps: `src/axioma/`.
- Credits stay in the footer: Pretext (MIT), herm of Hermes (Wikimedia Commons, CC0), the three typefaces (OFL).

## Motion: one conductor (A238)

- `assets/axioma/home.js` holds the motion conductor: one scheduler on one animation loop that owns every accent on the homepage. The story engine runs its drag frames on that same loop. The loop sleeps when nothing changes.
- One accent at a time. The section under a reading line 40% down the screen is the one being read; when it changes, the running accent settles first (300 ms at most), then the cursor hands over, then that section's accent plays after a 280 ms dwell, with 120 ms of stillness between accents.
- The accents, each with a cue and an end: the hero's block cursor blinks two or three times (1.1 s cycle), then holds (section prompts retired in A413, so the cursor no longer travels); the story's four signals light once in reading order on first arrival (420 ms each); the replay rows and the ledger numbers are final from the first paint (A413 retired the replay fade-in and the ledger count-up: a reader who jumps straight there, or a screenshot, must never see a dimmed or wrong figure; `replay-run` and `ledger-count` stay in the code as no-ops); the medallion ring breathes (1.4% scale, 6 s, on the compositor) only while the story is being read and the medallion is at rest. The gate's braille caduceus lives only while the gate is being read (A291, Percy approved): `assets/axioma/caduceus.js` draws it on a 68 x 72 dot grid with ordered dither; the wings lift on a 7.4 s breath, the right wing trailing the left, with a soft dither drifting across the feathers; each snake undulates, breathes, flicks its tongue and carries a warm glint on its own unrelated clocks; the staff never moves. It eases in over 1.6 s, redraws 24 times a second, and eases to rest over 0.9 s when the reader moves on. Loaded only near the gate and never under reduced motion; with no JavaScript the hand-drawn art in the HTML stands. The ring turns and the face tilts only while the medallion travels; no idle spin.
- Dragging or keying the medallion holds every accent; the ring breathes again 1.2 s after it rests. Pretext reflow and lighting on contact are unchanged.
- A295 adds five finite accents on that same queue, never beside the caduceus: map edges resolve from 42% to full opacity once (900 ms); Work and Limits headings resolve from 64% opacity once (620 ms); Method's 56px rule takes one shallow breath (2200 ms, 6% width); Catalog and Findings have one faint braille shimmer (2200 ms, 16–36% opacity); Work/Findings links acknowledge hover or focus with a 2px out-and-back drift (700 ms). New accents tick at most 30 fps and sleep after finishing. The reading-line IntersectionObserver retires them offscreen; document visibility cancels draws and wake timers while hidden. Reduced motion removes decoration and disables transitions entirely. STOP's bold weight is reserved in the story layer so lighting it cannot shift its baseline.
- A296 adds section-local ongoing life on that same queue. The diagram gets one brass pulse following orders down, evidence up, handoff and refusal edges (3.6 seconds per edge, 0–28% opacity, at most 30 draws/s). Run one order pauses that ambient clock, visits OWNER → OPERATOR → WORKERS, then axioma → gatekeeper → standing → tripwire on a refusal path → handydandy; each gate holds its unchanged panel for 1.5s, then evidence returns to OWNER. The example takes 13.35 active seconds, cancels via Cancel order/Escape/manual node selection, and replays. Hidden/offscreen progress freezes and resumes in place. Reduced motion instead exposes explicit Next step advances with no animation/timer. Switching motion preference cancels a run. New native controls are ≥44px and add only the explanatory line “An example order, not a live action.”
- Section dividers (outside the medallion story and reference gate) breathe at 12fps, 16–24% opacity, staggered 6.3–9.13s cycles; Method's short rule breathes 48–56% on its own 8.731s clock after its retained arrival. Only the reading section moves. Diagram divider breathing shares its pulse accent. No new ambient accent competes with the caduceus or changes caduceusLife(), gate placement or its timings. The engine, conductor function, gate markup and original panel copy are independently SHA-locked.
- A405 adds two accents on the same queue. The hero's mark breathes while the hero is read (`mark-breath`, ambient): each circle's cut overlay rises from 0 to at most 6% opacity, stone over night, on its own clock (9.1 s, 11.3 s, 13.7 s, phases 0 / 2.1 / 4.2 rad, raised-cosine), eased in over 1.6 s, settling in 300 ms, 12 draws a second; the triangle's edges are never drawn. `Construct` (`construct-run`, user-triggered, `#elements`) draws Proposition I.1 in 7.4 s of active time: the given line AB holds 600 ms, the circle about A is traced from B (1.6 s, 72 arc pieces), the circle about B from A (1.6 s from 2.5 s), C appears (600 ms from 4.4 s), CA and CB are drawn from C (1.2 s from 5.3 s), then a 900 ms hold; the step being drawn is lit in the list. Pieces change opacity only, 30 draws a second. Leaving the section freezes it in place and returning resumes it; switching to reduced motion finishes the figure at once. Under reduced motion the same button steps the figure by hand (`data-show` 0 to 4, `Next step`, `Construct again`) with no animation. Without JS the full figure and all steps stand and the button's space stays reserved but invisible.
- Opacity and transform only (the signal lights reuse the story's existing color state). Nothing flashes faster than once a second. Nothing is baked into the HTML: with no JavaScript or with reduced motion, the page is complete and still (cursor solid, numbers final, story static).
- Proof: `tests/motion.test.mjs` and `tests/browser/motion.test.mjs` (accents never overlap, every accent ends, one cursor on screen, layout shift 0 once motion runs, reduced motion off, drag holds accents).
